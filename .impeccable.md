## Design Context

### Users
Academics, research collaborators, potential employers, and industry contacts encountering Seongjun Lee for the first time. They're typically arriving from a CV link or LinkedIn. Context: desktop-first but must work on mobile. They're here to assess credibility, depth, and intellectual seriousness — not to be entertained.

### Brand Personality
Quiet confidence. Precise. Considered. Three words: **restrained, deliberate, rigorous.**

Emotional goal: the visitor should feel like they've encountered someone thoughtful — not someone trying to impress them. The design should communicate through absence as much as presence.

### Aesthetic Direction
Jony Ive / Steve Jobs minimalism. The user explicitly requested this. Key traits:
- Generous whitespace — content floats, never packs
- Typography-first: weight 600 max on headings, nothing shouts
- Blues only — a range from deep navy to sky. No purple, teal, pink, or indigo.
- Shadows are diffused and neutral (no colored shadows)
- Borders are near-invisible
- Animations are smooth, deliberate, never bouncy
- Background: animated canvas with blobs and wave sheets — atmosphere, not decoration
- Both light and dark mode supported with a toggle

Anti-references: agency portfolio flash, neon-on-dark "developer vibes", gradient text, bouncy spring physics, anything that feels like it's trying to be cool.

### Design Principles
1. **Restraint is the statement.** If something can be removed, it probably should be.
2. **Motion should be inevitable.** Transitions feel like physics, not effects.
3. **Blue is the only color.** Every hue is a shade of blue. No exceptions.
4. **Whitespace communicates confidence.** Dense layouts feel anxious; open layouts feel authoritative.
5. **Extraordinary is invisible.** The wow should be felt before it's noticed.
